Jeong-gi Kwak

I'm a postdoctoral research fellow at University of British Columbia (UBC), working with Prof. Kwang Moo Yi. My research interests are in 3D visual foundation models and image/video generative models, with the broader goal of understanding and modeling the visual world. Before joining UBC, I worked in industry startups. At NXN Labs, I developed large-scale generative foundation models for fashion imagery, and at Innerverz AI, I focused on video diffusion models. I received my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from Korea University, where I was advised by Prof. Hanseok Ko. I also completed my B.S. and M.S. degrees in Electrical Engineering at Korea University.
